# Marketing metrics cheat sheet (DTC and SaaS)

| Metric | Formula | Used for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CAC** (customer acquisition cost) | marketing spend ÷ new customers | Efficiency of acquisition | Use *new* customers only; decide whether to include salaries/tools ("fully loaded") |
| **ROAS** (return on ad spend) | attributed revenue ÷ ad spend | Paid media performance | Platform-reported ROAS often over-counts; compare with GA4 / blended |
| **Break-even ROAS** | 1 ÷ gross margin | Minimum ROAS to not lose money on the first order | Ignores repeat purchases, so it's conservative |
| **MER** (marketing efficiency ratio / blended ROAS) | total revenue ÷ total marketing spend | DTC health check that ignores attribution noise | Includes organic and repeat revenue |
| **AOV** (average order value) | revenue ÷ orders | Pricing, bundles, free-shipping thresholds | Skewed by big orders; check the median too |
| **Conversion rate** | conversions ÷ sessions (or visitors) | Landing page / funnel quality | Define the denominator consistently |
| **DTC LTV** (gross margin) | AOV × orders per year × years retained × gross margin | How much a customer is worth | Use margin, not revenue, when comparing to CAC |
| **SaaS LTV** | monthly ARPA × gross margin ÷ monthly churn | Value of a subscription account | Very sensitive to churn; small churn changes swing LTV a lot |
| **LTV:CAC** | LTV ÷ CAC | Can you afford to grow? | 3:1 is the common rule of thumb; > 5:1 can mean under-investing |
| **CAC payback** | CAC ÷ monthly gross profit per customer | Cash needed to fund growth | SaaS benchmark is often < 12 months |
| **Churn rate** | customers lost in period ÷ customers at start | Retention | Separate voluntary vs involuntary (failed payments) churn |
| **NPS** | % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6) | Customer loyalty / CX | Track the verbatim reasons, not just the score |
| **CTR** | clicks ÷ impressions | Creative / ad relevance | High CTR + low CVR usually means a landing page mismatch |
| **CPM / CPC / CPA** | cost per 1,000 impressions / per click / per acquisition | Media buying | Compare within the same channel and objective |

Benchmarks vary a lot by category, price point and stage, so treat them as starting points, not targets.
